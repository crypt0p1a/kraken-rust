# 0.1.0

First release of the Rust crates of the Kraken Spot REST API.

## kraken-spot-api

- The calls of the Kraken Spot REST API, grouped by domain: `market`, `transparency`, `account`,
  `trading`, `funding`, `earn`, `algo` and `subaccounts`
- `PublicApi` and `PrivateApi`, the facades over the clients of `kraken-network`
- Exact `Decimal` amounts and prices, parsed from the strings of Kraken

## kraken-network

- The public and private HTTP clients, the answers of Kraken and their errors
- A rate limiter shared by the clients of a same account, and the nonces of the private calls

## kraken-crypto

- The API secret, and the `API-Sign` signature of the private calls
